Marginalia - FAQ

Purpose: A distraction-free drafting environment for writers who draft from existing outlines and chapter cards.

Features

Import

Import manuscript files (TXT, MD) or chapter cards (TXT, MD).

Export

Export your manuscript locally as TXT or MD.

Themes

Switch between Dark Draft, Paper Draft, Manuscript, and E-Ink modes.

Marginalia is free and always will be. If it helped you finish a chapter, start a story, or spend a little more time writing than scrolling, and you'd like to support future development, feel free to reach out to the author.
